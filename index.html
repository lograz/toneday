Best with headphones How it works

Not a tracker. A canvas.

Toneday asks one question a day, without words: what color is today? There are no scores, no streaks and no charts. You move your finger, the ink spreads, and the sound changes with it.

At the end of the week your days blend into a single artwork with its own melody. Even the grey days make something beautiful.

Three moments

  1. Every day

    Choose a color

    Around the circle the color changes, toward the edge it grows more intense. Each color has its own sound, so you hear it while you choose.

  2. Sunday evening

    Watch it come together

    Your days fall like drops, one by one, and melt into an artwork that moves slowly and plays its own melody.

  3. All week after

    Keep it with you

    Save it as your phone wallpaper. The same seven colors never take the same shape twice.

Made to be gentle

No pressure

No streaks, no badges, no guilt. A skipped day becomes a pause in the music.

No numbers

No scores or statistics about your mood. Only color and sound.

Private by design

Your colors stay in your browser. No account and no ads. We only count anonymous visits, without cookies.

Made for everyone

Every color is described in words for screen readers, and every week can be listened to, not only seen.

Questions

Is Toneday free?

Yes, completely. No ads, no subscription, no account.

Where are my colors saved?

Only on your device, in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server. If you clear your browser data, your colors go with it.

Do I need headphones?

No, but they help. The sound is part of the artwork: with headphones each day has its own place around you, from Monday on the left to Sunday on the right.

Why does every color have a sound?

Each color plays one note of the same chord, so every week sounds harmonious. Warm colors sound rounder, blues more liquid, greens like wind, dark colors deeper. Nothing you choose can sound wrong.

Can I install it on my phone?

Yes. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen. On Android, choose Install app from the browser menu. It also works offline.

Is it a therapy or mental health app?

No. Toneday is a gentle daily ritual, not a medical or psychological tool. If you are going through a hard time, talking to someone can help: findahelpline.com lists free, confidential support near you.
