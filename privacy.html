Privacy

Short version

Toneday does not know who you are. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Your colors

The colors you choose are saved only in your browser’s local storage, on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Visit statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies and does not identify you: it records anonymous, aggregated data such as the page visited, the country, the browser and the referring site.

Fonts and other resources

Fonts and every other file are served from our own site. No content is loaded from third parties while you use Toneday.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. Like any web server, it processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages.
